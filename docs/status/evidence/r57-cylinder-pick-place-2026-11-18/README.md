# R5.7: pick and place of an upright cylinder (November 18, 2026)

**Status: implemented, experimental.** The backend-neutral pick-and-place
planner and executor handle a non-box object: an upright cylinder. It
passes on MuJoCo, PyBullet and Gazebo.

## What changed

- **`arm_pick_place.Box.shape`** (`box` or `cylinder`):
  - a cylinder keeps its bounding extents (diameter, diameter, length), so
    support, standoff and placement code are unchanged;
  - the planner's private scene draws it as a cylinder geom;
  - the grasp crosses its diameter, tried at four wrist yaws (any yaw
    grips a cylinder).
- **`urdf_pick_place.py`** reads loose upright cylinders from the world
  file (radius, length) besides boxes.
- **New world `arm_pick_place_can`:** `arm_pick_place_lab` with
  `pickup_can` (50 × 90 mm, 60 g) in place of the cube, with its MJCF
  generated by `gen_mjcf_worlds.py` (the generator reproduces the existing
  lab MJCF byte for byte), `sim_maps.yaml` and registry entries.
- **Planner test:** the cylinder geom, a 50 mm grasp width and an upright
  place height.

**MuJoCo's finger reading:**

- On PyBullet and Gazebo the fingers stop at the can's 25 mm radius.
- On MuJoCo they stop 4.7 mm further in, with the TCP within 2 mm of the
  can's axis. The cube gave the expected 20 mm, so the difference is the
  finger-mesh/cylinder contact.
- The grasp, unsupported hold and place still pass.

Receipts: `*-1.json`. Launch logs are on SSD under
`robot_lab_runtime/r57-can-20261118` (hashes here). The runs shared the
host with Go2 policy training.

## Limits

- Upright cylinders only. Lying cylinders, meshes and compound objects
  remain.
- Two runs on MuJoCo and PyBullet, one on Gazebo; Isaac is not run.
- Object poses come from simulator truth. The RGB-D estimator fits boxes
  only.
