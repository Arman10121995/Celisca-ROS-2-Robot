# Go2 stairs: curriculum fixed, traversal not passed (r1–r5, November 18–19, 2026)

**Status: not passed** (implemented, experimental).

R5.2's done-when asks for a stairs traversal with tracked contact and
effort. No trained or vendor Go2 policy passes it yet.

## Training

| Run | Start | Iterations | Curriculum | Final level (rise) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r1 | `go2_rough_r1` iteration 2500 | to 5205 (stopped) | 3 m from spawn | 0.15 (4.4 cm) from iteration 3500 on |
| r2 | r1 iteration 5205 | 3000, to 8205 (100.8 M steps) | path ≥ 80 % of commanded distance | 0.5 (10 cm) |

Both runs used the walk task, 512 environments, 20 s episodes,
randomisation, observation noise and 20 ms latency, on CPU. Pyramid stairs
with 0.3 m treads, rising 2–18 cm with the curriculum.

**Why r1 stalled:** its rule (promote above 3 m from spawn, demote below
1.5 m) is rarely met. Random turning, sideways and slow commands seldom
ask for 3 m, so the level stayed at 0.15.

**r2's rule:** each episode now records its commanded distance and the
base's walked path. The level rises when robots cover ≥ 80 % of the
commanded distance and falls below 40 %. Episodes commanding under 1 m do
not count. r2 rose to 0.4 quickly, held there for about 2000 iterations,
then reached 0.5 (`curriculum-curve.json`).

**Training-environment evaluation** (`evaluate_walk_*.json`):

- flat: 0.5 m/s → 0.57 m/s, 0.3 → 0.32, spin 0.8 → 0.69 rad/s, no falls;
- 18 cm stairs: 0.5 → 0.19 m/s.

An earlier checkpoint (5650) ignored 0.3 m/s commands; the final policy
tracks them.

## Screens in a normal MuJoCo launch

`scripts/screen_stairs.py` (new):

- drives forward and judges by truth: rise above start, whole steps
  climbed, falls (tilt > 60°);
- traces efforts from `/joint_states` and foot contacts from
  `/go2/foot_contact_forces`.

Launch: `robot_model:=unitree_go2 simulator:=mujoco mode:=loc
legged_policy:=<dir> spawn_x:=-7.5 spawn_y:=-4.0`.

**Spawn:** x = −7.8 puts the rear legs inside a wall at x = −8.1 (the
`early` and `himloco` screens, kept). −7.5 matches the October trials.

**New world `terrain_stairs_low`:** the same arena with 10 cm rises, the
height the curriculum reached. It is generated by `gen_terrain_arenas.py
--only terrain_stairs_low` and registered in `sim_maps.yaml` and the
registry.

| Policy | World (rise) | Command | Forward | Max rise | Steps | Fell | Front-left foot in contact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| vendor `go2_himloco` | `terrain_stairs` (18 cm) | 0.3 m/s | 0.15 m | 4.3 cm | 0 | no | — |
| r2 checkpoint 5650 | 18 cm | 0.3 m/s | 0.00 m | 0 | 0 | no | — |
| r2 checkpoint 5650 | 18 cm | 0.5 m/s | 0.31 m | 4.4 cm | 0 | no | — |
| **r2 final**, run 1 | 18 cm | 0.5 m/s | 0.47 m | 3.5 cm | 0 | no | 3 % |
| **r2 final**, run 2 | 18 cm | 0.5 m/s | 0.44 m | 3.3 cm | 0 | no | 3 % |
| **r2 final** | `terrain_stairs_low` (10 cm) | 0.5 m/s | 0.38 m | 2.9 cm | 0 | no | 1 % |

**What happens:**

- Every policy walks to the first step and stops there without falling.
- On the low stairs the trained policy gets its front feet onto the first
  step, but the base never rises.
- The trained policy keeps its front-left foot in the air almost all the
  time, a three-legged gait, in every screen. That points to a training
  problem, not the step height alone.

Peak efforts and contact fractions are in each `screen-*.json`. Traces here
are thinned to 1 Hz; full traces and launch logs are on SSD under
`robot_lab_runtime/go2-stairs-screen-20261118`.

## The three-legged gait is inherited

Foot contact on flat `nav_empty` at 0.5 m/s, 10 s (`flat-*.json`; feet in
order FL, FR, RL, RR):

| Policy | Forward | Fraction of time in contact |
|---|---|---|
| `go2_walk_r3` (flat) | 5.07 m | 0.53, 0.49, 0.49, 0.50: an even trot |
| `go2_rough_r1` (rough; the start of the stairs runs) | 5.45 m | **0.16**, 0.64, 0.55, 0.48 |
| `go2_stairs_r2` | 3.54 m | **0.15**, 0.08, 0.76, 0.25 |

The rough-terrain run already favoured its front-left foot, and stairs
training made it worse. Stairs r3 therefore resumes from `go2_walk_r3`.

## Stairs r3 (from the even trot): still not passed

r3 resumed `go2_walk_r3` (iteration 2000) with the same settings and the
commanded-distance curriculum:

- 3000 iterations, to 5000;
- level 0.7 (13 cm) within 300 iterations, held to the end
  (`r3-curriculum-curve.json`);
- on flat ground it walks 4.1 m in 10 s and its gait stays close to even
  (contact 0.39 / 0.61 / 0.70 / 0.49).

**Changes to the course and the screen:**

- **Run-up:** `terrain_stairs_low`'s stairs now start at x = −6.2, 1 m
  further east, for a 1.3 m run-up. In the original arena the stairs begin
  0.8 m from the west wall. Spawned at the riser, r3 froze on both worlds
  (`screen-low-r3-1`, `screen-r3-1`, `screen-*-765`). On open floor in the
  same world it walks 4.8 m (`screen-low-r3-openfloor`).
- **`--hold-line`:** the screen can now steer like an operator, holding
  the start line and heading. Without it r3 drifted 0.6 m sideways and
  walked up beside the 1.1 m-wide strip (`screen-low2-go2_stairs_r3`: 5 m
  forward, base height unchanged).
- **Step count:** the tolerance is now 1 cm (was 3 cm). With the front feet
  on a step, the base pitches up most of a rise without climbing it.

**Result** (`screen-low3-go2_stairs_r3`, 10 cm stairs, holding the line):

- r3 walks straight to the first step and puts its front feet on it (base
  rise 8.2 cm).
- It then stands frozen for the remaining 35 s, with its front-left foot
  lifted.
- Not a climb.

r2 on the shifted course paws at the first step (`screen-low2-go2_stairs_r2`).

## Stairs r4 (riser starts): not passed

r4 resumed r3's final checkpoint (iteration 5000) for 2000 iterations with
one trainer change: 30 % of stairs episodes start 0.3 m before a random
riser, facing it (± 0.2 rad).

- The curriculum restarted at level 0 and reached 0.4 (8.4 cm) by
  iteration 6200, then stayed there to 7000 (`r4-curriculum-curve.json`).
  r3 had reached 0.7.
- **Screen** (`screen-low3-go2_stairs_r4`, 10 cm stairs, 0.5 m/s, holding
  the line):
  - it walked 0.76 m in 8 s, then stood at x = −6.65, 0.45 m short of the
    first riser, for the remaining 32 s;
  - max rise 4.1 cm, no fall, feet almost always in contact
    (0.98 / 0.77 / 0.75 / 0.99).
- Worse than r3, which at least reached the step.

**The policies are blind.** Every run here observes angular velocity,
gravity, commands, joint positions and velocities and the last action, and
no terrain. legged_gym's rough and stairs configurations add a height scan
around the base (`measure_heights`). A blind policy can only find a riser
with its feet.

## Stairs r5 (height scan): walks to the riser and keeps trying; not passed

r5 resumed r3 (iteration 5000) with one change: the height-scan
observation (legged_gym's 17 × 11 grid, 0.1 m spacing). The blind
checkpoint was widened with zero weights on the 187 new inputs. Riser
starts were off.

- 3000 iterations, to 8000. The curriculum reached level 0.5 (10 cm, the
  course height) by iteration 5900 and stayed there
  (`r5-curriculum-curve.json`).
- In the training environment it tracks 0.5 m/s as 0.73 m/s on flat ground.

**First screens failed because of a deployment bug** (kept as `*-failed-scan-misses`):

- the robot stood still on flat ground and on the course;
- about 10 of the 187 deployed scan points read 1000 m, the "nothing
  there" value;
- under the robot, the MuJoCo ray met the trunk, and the re-cast started
  0.1 mm below the hit, inside the robot. It met the robot again until its
  4 tries ran out.

Rays now step 2 cm past a robot hit, with up to 40 tries. A test casts
under the real Go2: with the old settings it gives 10 misses, now none.

**Screens after the fix:**

| Screen | Result |
|---|---|
| `nav_empty`, 0.5 m/s, 15 s (`flat-r5-2`) | 5.97 m, no fall; feet in contact 0.81 / 0.38 / 0.31 / 0.71 |
| `terrain_stairs_low`, 0.5 m/s, holding the line (`screen-low3-go2_stairs_r5`) | not passed: walks 0.85 m to the first riser, then keeps trying for 35 s; the base rises 5–7 cm and settles back, max rise 7.1 cm, 0 steps, no fall |

Unlike the blind runs, it keeps stepping at the riser instead of freezing.
It trained no higher than the course's 10 cm. r6 continues from r5 with
the height scan and riser starts (30 %).

## Stairs r6 (height scan + riser starts): not passed

r6 resumed r5 (iteration 8000) for 3000 iterations with `--height-scan
--riser-start 0.3`, again with 512 environments on the CPU while other
work shared the host.

- **Curriculum:** level 0.5 (10 cm) from iteration 8600 to the end;
  mean return fell from 12.1 to 6.0 (`r6-curriculum-curve.json`).
- **`terrain_stairs_low`, 0.5 m/s, holding the line
  (`screen-low3-go2_stairs_r6`):** walks 0.87 m to the first riser and
  steps at it for 40 s. The base rises at most 8.5 cm (r5: 7.1 cm), 0
  steps, no fall.
- **`nav_empty`, 0.5 m/s, 15 s (`flat-r6`):** 5.72 m, no fall. Base height
  varies up to 8.7 cm (r5: 2 cm), and only the front-right and rear-left
  feet were in contact at the end sample.

Riser starts with terrain input did not get past the 10 cm level either.
The stairs time-box is closed again after r6.

## Next

  episodes (r4) did not help.
- Height scan: implemented on MuJoCo (r5); r5 tries at the riser but
  does not climb; r6 (plus riser starts) does not either. Other backends
  need their own scan publisher.
- Train with treads like the course (1.1 m) or a mixed tread curriculum.
- GPU-scale training (thousands of environments) is not available on this
  host.
