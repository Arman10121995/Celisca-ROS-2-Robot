# R10 multi-leg implementation — October 15, 2026

Baseline `8ede6f2`, Jetson/aarch64, ROS 2 Humble, MuJoCo 3.12.0. R10.6/R10.7/
R10.8 remain **partial**. Three complete models, native Gait/Drive workflow,
matching commands/defaults, source IK, tripod/tetrapod/ripple/wave schedules,
bounded in-step PD, actual contact/tilt observations and interruption guards
are implemented. [Operator/agent guide](../tutorials/multi-leg-gaits.md).

Normal GUI runs in `nav_empty` observe forward inputs and held Stop on each
model: approximately 0.0974 m hexapod, 0.1760 m octopod and 0.125 m centipede
at the six-second input-end samples (the later centipede run measures 0.1235 m).
Their receipt records exact source hashes
per run. Timestamp/reset barriers were subsequently added and the centipede
run repeated on that source; the final inactive-Stop reason preservation and
static-preview improvements do not constitute another physical qualification.

Actual source masses and physical contacts are checked separately from
runtime missions. The first octopod's massless root lost declared trunk mass
during URDF import, producing about 11.31 kg instead of 4.64 kg. The derivative
now has a massive trunk root with an identity footprint child; measured stance
support settles to about 45.52 N. The original imported CAD remains preserved.
Eight focused tests check source chains/IK, compiled masses/effort bounds,
actual miniature contact forces and excluding the robot from floor placement.
The hexapod mass assertion initially failed at 0.0001007 kg because child-body
serialization uses six significant digits; its explicit rounding bound is
recorded, and the primitive models retain tight mass tolerance.

Failed first spawn-string arithmetic, the superseded octopod mass/cleanup
diagnostic, original source hashes and raw measurements remain on SSD. The
later octopod, hexapod and centipede runs exit cleanly. These are short physical
implementation diagnostics, not terrain, recovery, contact-free or all-map
walking mission certificates. Independent sensor-rate, estimate, saved SLAM,
navigation, other-backend and physical joystick work remain open.

[Machine-readable receipt](gaits-receipt-2026-10-15.json) and
[artifact handoff](evidence/r106-gaits-2026-10-15/README.md) distinguish measured
source revisions from the final published candidate. The GUI's experimental
Gait mode is available; ground higher-mode gates remain closed for these models.
