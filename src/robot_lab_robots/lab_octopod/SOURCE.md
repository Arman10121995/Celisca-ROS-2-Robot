# Laboratory octopod

Complete first-party Apache-2.0 eight-legged robot generated by
`scripts/generate_gait_assets.py`: eight distinct three-joint chains, inertias,
collision boxes/rubber foot spheres, limits and 24 bounded physical PD motors.
Default alternating tetrapod, ripple and wave are explicit schedules rather
than a padded six-leg policy. Body mass 2.4 kg plus 0.28 kg per leg totals
4.64 kg. The massive trunk root preserves source inertia through import.

Authored educational joint PD: 75 Nm/rad, 2.5 Nms/rad, 8 Nm bounds and
0.01 kg m² reflected rotor inertia. Initial coxa/femur/tibia [0, 0.3, 1.1] rad.
No hardware calibration is claimed. KDL conversion is reused from the pinned
MIT hexapod source, with notice retained in `robot_lab_utils/vendor`; geometry
and adapter are separately authored.

Initial MuJoCo Display/Gait and GUI Drive/feedback are implemented. Broader
terrain/contact/loss/reset, independent estimation, SLAM/navigation and
other backend integration remain pending.
