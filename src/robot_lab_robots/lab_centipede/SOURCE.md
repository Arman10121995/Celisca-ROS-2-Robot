# Laboratory centipede

Complete first-party Apache-2.0 six-segment, twelve-legged robot generated by
`scripts/generate_gait_assets.py`. Six 0.8 kg body masses and five actual bounded
yaw spine joints connect twelve distinct 0.28 kg three-joint legs: complete
source mass 8.16 kg and 41 physical actuators. Segments/limbs remain under this
one complete family.

Twelve-foot wave/ripple schedules and limited propagated spine wave/curvature
use source-local leg IK and bounded physical in-step PD. Bodies are never
animated to imitate locomotion. Gains/rotor inertia match the explicitly
authored octopod design, with 6 Nm spine and 8 Nm leg bounds. The KDL conversion
retains the upstream MIT notice; this geometry and adapter are authored.

MuJoCo Display/Gait and GUI Drive/actual contact/tilt feedback are the initial
scope. Stable articulated terrain walking, tail clearance, recovery, measured
missions, independent estimation, SLAM/navigation and other backends remain
pending. This is not a named hardware robot or a twelve-leg learned policy.
