# Laboratory ROV provenance and physical contract

October 15 continuation: the derived added-mass diagonals are positive for
Fortress 6.18's **−Ma × acceleration** law. Signed linear/quadratic damping
stays negative; Coriolis/added mass remain enabled. The new tank GUI receipt
in `docs/status/auv-receipt-2026-10-15.json` measures the corrected source.
The original ROV receipt remains historical, with its exact prior source.

First-party Robot Lab model, Apache-2.0; generated by
`scripts/generate_marine_assets.py`. This is a complete authored laboratory
vehicle, not a calibrated BlueROV/Tethys model or a hardware specification.
The hull, inertia, eight rotor links/joints, camera/IMU and native plugin
bindings belong to one robot family; individual rotors are not catalog robots.

The hull collision is 0.4 × 0.28 × 0.16 m, with 18.4 kg hull mass and hull
centre of mass 0.025 m below its collision-volume centre. Each rotor link is
0.01 kg. The source OAK-D camera contributes its own mass/collision volume.
`MarineModel` reads the expanded source, computes total mass/displacement,
joint-origin/axis allocation and signed thrust bounds, and checks rank six.
Every force is bounded to ±12 N in both controller allocation and the native
thruster plugin. Allocation uniformly scales a saturated wrench rather than
clipping its components into a different direction.

Four diagonal horizontal rotors control surge/sway/yaw. Four vertical corner
rotors control heave/roll/pitch. Gazebo's native Thruster system spins joints
and applies forces; Buoyancy applies water/air density by collision volume,
and Hydrodynamics applies authored drag/added-mass/Coriolis/current terms.
Thrust coefficient 0.0044, 0.08 m diameter, damping and controller gains are
educational parameters, not experimentally calibrated curves. The physics
must be measured before hardware or mission claims are made.

The current observer uses actual simulator body poses, stamped finite
differences and quaternion orientation. Ideal pressure is explicitly emulated
from that pose with `101325 + density × 9.81 × depth`; it is not an independent
pressure sensor or GPS. Actual native IMU and RGB-D are bridged separately.
Independent DVL/sonar/pressure estimation, saved SLAM and navigation remain
unimplemented. Only native Gazebo Fortress Display/Marine is declared.

Primary physics references: [Fortress underwater systems](https://gazebosim.org/api/gazebo/6/underwater_vehicles.html),
[native thruster source](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/blob/ign-gazebo6/src/systems/thruster/Thruster.cc).
Source system binaries are installed separately; none is vendored here.
